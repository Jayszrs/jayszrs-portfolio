Each service page contains its actual scope, enquiry preparation, project process, answers to common questions, a working Contact action, and links to existing portfolio evidence. No invented prices, reviews, response times, or ranking guarantees.

Titles, descriptions, unique canonicals, Open Graph URLs, Person/Service/WebPage/BreadcrumbList data, footer links, and the ten-page sitemap connect these services to Jaelani Surya Saputra / Jay Szrs in Jakarta and Bekasi. Keywords appear in readable content, not hidden keyword blocks.

Production inspection found `www.jayszrs.my.id` redirects to `jayszrs.my.id` while previous canonicals pointed to `www`. The shared URL helper now uses the active apex hostname and normalizes the old environment value. The old www Search Console sitemap entry showed a failed fetch and zero discovered pages; this is historical Search Console data, not proof that the currently accessible sitemap is broken.

The existing owner-selected dragon vector remains the brand source. `scripts/render-brand-icons.cjs` exports 32/48/192/512px PNGs, Apple touch icon, a real three-frame ICO, and a separately padded maskable PWA icon. Google may refresh its displayed favicon after recrawling; displaying it is Google's decision.

Validation: optimized Next.js 16.4.2 builds pass. `verify-services-seo.cjs` passes ten unique canonical/OG URLs, initial crawler HTML, linked Service schema, real Person image, admin noindex, sitemap, favicon dimensions/ICO structure, manifest, FAQ interactions, and 320/390/768/1440px layouts with zero browser page errors. Search Console submission and live deployment results are recorded separately after publication. Indexing requests are not proof of indexing or a top ranking.
